## officialRuleResearch

The mandatory sources establish the following facts.

### Phase boundaries and day

- Rules Explanation describes day/night phases, day conversation, and the first
  nomination procedure.
- Players may vote as few or as many times as they choose during a day.
- The Glossary defines Dawn as the end of night immediately before the next day,
  Day as eyes-open talk/vote time, and each day as followed by night and each
  night by day.
- Dawn wakes all players; the next day follows.

### Nomination

- Only alive players nominate.
- Each player can nominate at most once per day.
- Each player can be nominated once per day.
- Dead players can be nominated.
- Only one nomination is active at a time.
- Defense follows the nomination and then the vote.

### Voting and execution

- An alive player may vote any number of times in a day.
- A dead player has one remaining vote token, which is consumed when counted.
- A candidate succeeds only with at least half of living players, strictly more
  votes than every other candidate, and at least one vote.
- A tie means that nobody is executed.
- Votes are tallied clockwise, ending at the nominee.
- At most one execution occurs per day, and there may be none.
- The official Glossary and States distinguish execution from death: a player
  may be executed and remain alive; execution still counts as the daily
  execution even when the target does not die; a dead player cannot die again.

### Chinese source facts

- 首页 distinguishes day and night.
- 提名 states the nomination limits, alive-only nominators, dead-eligible
  nominees, and the one-active-nomination boundary.
- 投票 states the living/dead vote rules and the threshold/strict-greatest
  requirements.
- 处决 explicitly distinguishes death caused by execution from merely being
  executed and records role exceptions that can make execution occur without
  death.
- 夜晚行动顺序一览 provides first-night and other-night lists and allows
  either the original official order or its recommended alternative. It is not
  selected as canonical order against the official nightsheet.

## firstNightOrder

The official nightsheet is canonical for the night-order boundary. It contains
`80` first-night entries, with dawn at zero-based index `77` (ordinal `78`).
The first night begins the game, ends at dawn, wakes all players, and is
followed by the first day. No source defines an engine-level task-completion
predicate; a later design must not claim external rule authority for internal
task settlement.

## otherNightOrder

The official nightsheet contains `99` other-night entries, with dawn at
zero-based index `97` (ordinal `98`). Near the boundary it records:

- first night: `cultleader, spy, ogre, highpriestess, general, chambermaid,
  mathematician, dawn`;
- other night: `butler, spy, highpriestess, general, chambermaid, mathematician,
  riot, dawn`.

The Chinese order page is treated as localization/context only because it
explicitly permits an alternative order; no alternative is silently selected.

## interactions

The sourced interaction matrix is:

1. First-night to first-day: first night begins the game; night ends at dawn;
   dawn wakes all; the next day follows.
2. Day start: dawn is the boundary and eyes open; no additional public rule is
   mandated by the sources.
3. Nomination: alive nominate; each player may nominate once per day; each
   player may be nominated once per day; dead may be nominated; one nomination
   is active at a time.
4. Voting: alive may vote repeatedly; a dead player has one remaining vote;
   the candidate needs at least half of living players, strictly the greatest
   count, and at least one vote; ties fail; count clockwise ending at nominee.
5. Execution: daily maximum one, possibly none; after normal execution the day
   ends.
6. Execution/death: execution and death are distinct; execution need not kill.
7. Day to ordinary night: each day is followed by night; the sources do not
   define an engine-level counter/reset or task-settlement predicate.
8. Ordinary night to dawn: the official nightsheet supplies canonical order
   and the dawn boundary.
9. Ghost vote/dead nomination: dead cannot nominate, may be nominated, and have
   only one vote token remaining.
10. Win condition is not required for a basic loop unless a fixture reaches a
    terminal state.

## unresolvedConflicts

### Execution/death conflict

The Chinese 投票 oldid 5936 broadly states that at the end of the nomination
phase the current about-to-die player is “executed and dies”. The official BOTC
Glossary oldid 2874 and States oldid 1039 explicitly distinguish execution from
death and allow execution without death. Chinese 处决 oldid 6420 also explicitly
distinguishes them and records role exceptions that can make execution occur
without death.

This is a substantive conflict because the required 2C execution/death boundary
could be exercised by a fixture involving death prevention or execution without
death. The corpus does not provide one unambiguous qualification for such a
fixture. No interpretation is selected.

`RULE_CONFLICT`

Conservative consequence: the proposed 2C slice is `HUMAN_BLOCKED` until rule
authority resolves the conflict or the fixture is strictly scoped to exclude
the ambiguity and that scope is recorded. No architect or implementer may
proceed from this evidence.

## Authority boundary

The sole supported chain is:

```text
SOURCE_HEAD_S -> PROFILE_MATERIALIZATION_P -> EVIDENCE_CUSTODY_E
```

1. S supplies already-existing exact source and governance inputs.
2. P may materialize the bounded ownership/routing/coverage baseline/profile
   from S. P cannot contain a future P self-identity, a future E identity, or
   evidence derived only after P exists.
3. E may conditionally preserve evidence about an already-existing P. E cannot
   become product/profile authority, modify P, contain its own future identity,
   or write evidence back into P.
4. Independent exact-head review remains external and later than the head it
   reviews. This rule-research record is not such a review.

The chain does not authorize history amendment, rebase, force-push,
self-referential hashing, manifest recursion, future-object prediction, or a
third D1.5 design correction.

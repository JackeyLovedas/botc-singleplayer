## 3. Corrected evidence-authority boundary

### 3.1 Design records expectations, not future facts

Every repository document authored before P or E exists may state only `EXPECTED_EVIDENCE_CONTRACT`. It may freeze schemas, field meanings, lifecycle transitions, commands, allowlists, and failure conditions. It must not claim an actual future P or E object ID, actual E parentage, actual P-to-E diff, actual absence of a later child, actual exact-E audit result, actual review result, actual review support ID, or final acceptance.

P is the unique artifact authority. Its accepted-profile candidate tree contains the versioned baseline/profile, stable accepted-profile validator, static selector, routing/profile records, and frozen D1.5 artifacts. P does not reference E, an E path as an artifact dependency, an E object ID, a post-E result, or an external review.

E is evidence authority only. E archives already-existing P identities, direct P artifact identities and hashes, and canonical preimages produced before E is committed. E does not generate, select, mutate, replace, reinterpret, or accept an artifact. E does not contain its own object ID, a predicted identity, a self-digest, an actual assertion about `parent(E)`, an actual assertion about the P-to-E diff, a no-Q assertion, an exact-E audit result, or a review result.

### 3.2 Non-self-referential lifecycle

The only repository lifecycle tokens are:

```text
P=MATERIALIZED_PENDING_EVIDENCE
E=EVIDENCE_COMMITTED_PENDING_EXTERNAL_REVIEW
```

`PROFILE_CHILD_P`, `ACCEPTED_1712_PROFILE`, and `ACCEPTED_1712_V1` are frozen identifiers inherited from D1.5. The word `ACCEPTED` inside an identifier is not a lifecycle fact. Identifier equality and lifecycle status are separate fields and must be validated independently. Neither P nor E may record `ACCEPTED` as its own current lifecycle.

Only a complete repository-external final record created after E exists may record:

```text
D1_5_LIFECYCLE=ACCEPTED
```

That transition is legal only when the real exact-E structural audit passes, the fresh independent report names the actual E head, both required review verdicts pass, `remainingBlockers=[]`, every retained D1.5 criterion is closed, and the current branch still ends at E. The record is never written back to P or E. Any unresolved `PENDING_EXTERNAL_REVIEW` row makes `ACCEPTED` illegal.

### 3.3 Reachable chronology

The only accepted chronology is:

```text
S exists
  -> P is committed once as direct single-parent child of S
  -> P lifecycle is MATERIALIZED_PENDING_EVIDENCE
  -> P-bound preimages and evidence bundle bytes are rendered without changing P
  -> E is committed once as direct single-parent docs/evidence child of P
  -> E lifecycle is EVIDENCE_COMMITTED_PENDING_EXTERNAL_REVIEW
  -> the exact-E structural audit runs against actual E
  -> a fresh independent reviewer binds actual E outside the repository tree
  -> the external final record may record ACCEPTED
```

There is no backward edge. P never references E. E never references itself. The external audit and review never write back. No amend, replacement object disguised under the same identity, or current-acceptance Q child exists.

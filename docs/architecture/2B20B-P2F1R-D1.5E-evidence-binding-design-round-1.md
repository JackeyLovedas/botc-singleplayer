## 7. No-cycle proof

The only dependency edges are:

```text
S OID and pre-P artifact bytes -> P tree
P OID and direct P artifact hashes -> E tree
E OID -> repository-external review record
```

The proof is closed:

1. S exists before P and contains no future P or E identity.
2. P exists before the P-bound manifest and contains no P self-ID, E identity, E path dependency, or post-P manifest SHA in its artifact-authority set.
3. The P-bound manifest is generated after P exists.
4. E records P and direct concrete-artifact hashes; E records no E identity or E-derived self-digest.
5. Git computes E's OID after E's parent/tree are fixed; E's tree does not contain that OID.
6. The external reviewer records actual E only after E exists; nothing is written back.

A direct SHA-256 of a concrete artifact is allowed. A digest whose only payload is another digest, a wrapper whose only payload is another manifest, an E self-digest, a snapshot-of-snapshot chain, a reserved future OID, or any backward write is forbidden.

## 8. Closed failure contract

## 14. Acceptance sequence and local gates

A future separately authorized implementation must execute this order and no other:

1. Verify the exact approved design/rule-evidence hashes, clean worktree, and available S Git object.
2. Verify S is the authorized D1.5 source head and all inherited D1.5 inputs are complete.
3. Pre-render and validate the exact P tree before commit.
4. Commit P once as the direct single-parent child of S; do not amend it.
5. Verify the P allowlist, accepted artifact hashes/counts, stable verifier, one selector replacement, and zero migration-only executable branch.
6. Capture and freeze P OID.
7. Generate the canonical P-bound manifest outside the repository using the exact S verifier blob and direct P bytes; run no coverage.
8. Re-read P OID and tree and require no change.
9. Pre-render and validate all three E files, including the complete six-row traceability ledger.
10. Commit E once as the direct single-parent child of P; do not amend it.
11. Run the section 9 positive checks and all 20 hostile cases against clones/temp worktrees without mutating S, P, or E.
12. Run the existing stable profile validation for the exact accepted profile, the D1.5E structural/traceability audits, `pnpm typecheck`, and `pnpm lint` only if dependencies are already present and the implementation authorization requires them.
13. Do not run coverage, ownership publication, Hosted CI, D2, or a workflow.
14. Freeze E and request one fresh independent complete code/rule review at exact E.
15. Require `CODE_REVIEW_PASS`, `RULE_REVIEW_PASS`, and `remainingBlockers=[]` from that one complete reviewer output before any later publication request.
16. Make no commit after a passing review.

Ordinary product tests may be run only if the later implementation authorization explicitly includes them; they cannot replace the structural evidence gates. D1.5E itself adds no test and claims no Hosted CI or cross-platform execution.

## 15. CI and cross-platform determinism

D1.5E modifies no workflow and runs no Hosted CI. It inherits no CI result from S, P, main, D1, or D1.5. A future D2/publication slice must obtain its own exact-head CI under fresh authorization.

All E evidence bytes are deterministic across Windows/Linux checkouts: UTF-8, LF, no BOM, exactly one terminal LF, POSIX repository paths, ordinal ordering, lowercase hexadecimal hashes, no timestamps in hashed material, no random values, no absolute worktree paths, no `localeCompare`, no `Intl`, and no environment-dependent separator. A CRLF checkout may be observed, but canonical Git-blob/LF bytes remain the hash authority.

## 17. Rollback and retry boundary

S, P, and E are immutable once emitted. Never amend, rebase, force-move, or rewrite them to backfill evidence.

- Failure before P commit: delete only OS-temporary output and retry from unchanged S.
- Failure after an unaccepted P commit but before E: preserve that P as unaccepted evidence; a separately authorized implementation repair may create a fresh replacement P as another direct child of the same S on a fresh unpublished repair branch. It may not amend the failed P.
- Failure after an unaccepted E commit: preserve that E as unaccepted evidence; a separately authorized implementation repair may create a fresh replacement E as a direct child of the accepted P on a fresh unpublished repair branch. It may not add a Q child, amend E, or treat the old E as accepted.
- Failure after a passing external review: any later commit invalidates the review. Stop and request new authority; do not inherit the old verdict.
- Rollback of an accepted local candidate is additive and explicit: abandon the unaccepted branch and retain P artifacts/history for audit. Historical 1572/D1 records and old profiles are never rewritten.

Retries are allowed only before a commit or under an authorized repair from the same immutable parent. A retry must reproduce byte-identical deterministic outputs from identical inputs.

## 19. Stop conditions

Stop immediately with `HUMAN_BLOCKED` or `RESLICE_REQUIRED` as applicable if:

- S is absent, dirty, wrong, or lacks required migration evidence;
- P cannot be a direct single-parent child of S;
- P requires a third changed path, more than one profile, more than one selector replacement, or retained migration-only code;
- any P artifact depends on E, future review, or post-P evidence;
- E cannot be a direct single-parent child of P;
- E needs a fourth file, executable, script, workflow, profile, selector, production, test, rule, or role-matrix change;
- E contains its own OID/self-digest or a later value;
- direct P/profile/delta/inventory/tuple hashes or exact counts disagree;
- P or E must be amended, rewritten, or followed by Q to repair evidence;
- a hash-of-hash, manifest-of-manifest, recursive snapshot, dynamic graph, mutable pointer, or permanent framework is proposed;
- lifecycle cleanup cannot remove migration-only assets without weakening stable validation;
- external review cannot bind exact E without writeback;
- D2 must start or consume E as artifact authority;
- any BOTC source conflict appears, role coverage changes, or product behavior changes;
- any budget is exceeded;
- the fresh independent design review is not `RULE_DESIGN_PASS` or has nonempty blockers.
